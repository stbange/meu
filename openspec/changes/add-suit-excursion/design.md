# Restricted suit route
The engineering wall and matching hull aperture open onto a 16 m wrist platform (X14..30, Z-12..-8, deck112). A monumental fastener and service conduit preserve human scale. All walking uses the existing first-person capsule/floor/ray paths, without teleports for egress. A barrier at X13.7 rejects crossing without a suit. Site alignment is X+/-15, Z-120..-175, yaw+/-0.05; main energy >=800 and heat<=6000, completed repair and scan/field are required.

Scan reports the authored envelope: 80 kPa, -10 C, 0.2 mSv/h, low debris. Activating shelter costs200 kJ/100heat; suit lasts180 active seconds. Outside shelter demand is2 kJ/s. Missing field power, removal of the source without bypass, excess heat or insufficient energy accumulates600 exposure ticks before catastrophe. The HUD gives seconds, exposure and return direction. One conductor/one sealant plus50 kJ/20heat provides the persistent parallel bypass. Extraction without it is warned, allowed and hazardous. The unit cannot travel while the suit is active.

Egress captures a secure checkpoint of stock, discoveries and power only after helper work is settled. Catastrophe freezes movement and offers explicit recovery; no automatic teleport occurs. Recovery returns to the inside airlock, restores checkpoint resources/discoveries, loses unsecured salvage, and preserves the established home. Saved outside state retains suit time, field, bypass, exposure and checkpoint; restored games remain paused. Schema2 includes fixed-width checkpoint fields and validates both live and checkpoint state. Visual and collision geometry are shared; no general external terrain simulation.
