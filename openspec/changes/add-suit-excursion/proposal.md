## Why
M1d provides precise recovery in a physically reachable service recess while making return safety understandable.
## What Changes
- Open a human-scale wrist airlock, railed platform and sheltered service conduit at engineering height.
- Require parked alignment, scanned pressure/temperature/radiation/debris/field conditions, repair, energy and thermal margin.
- Add suit timer, exposure warnings, shelter bypass, coupler extraction and explicit checkpoint recovery.
- Persist suit/exterior/checkpoint state through native and browser saves.
## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `suit-egress`: conditional egress, return protection and deliberate recovery.
### Modified Capabilities
None.
## Impact
Depends on M1a–M1c. One authored site; robot stays parked during egress. No unrestricted exterior world. Human acceptance deferred through M1.
