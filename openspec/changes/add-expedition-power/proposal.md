## Why
M1a needs useful power planning and an understandable alien supply before repair and salvage.

## What Changes
- Add stored power, sunlight, parked collectors, heat/cooling priorities and protected life support.
- Add first-person chart and engineering stations with desktop and touch actions.
- Migrate M0 saves into a versioned power-aware record while retaining recovery/export/import.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `mech-power`: bounded active-time power, collectors, priorities and return planning.
- `alien-grid`: parked connection, observable pulses and overload isolation.
### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact
M1a under the user-authorized deferral of human reviews through M1. No generated city or physical balancing. Numerical rates are provisional tuning.
