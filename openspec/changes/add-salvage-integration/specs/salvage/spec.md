## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: SALV-01 Unique recovery and atomic stock
A reachable parked manipulator SHALL recover a unique bulk-stock cradle only with a working bus and sufficient energy/capacity. Common repair stock SHALL be obtainable without the discovered technology.
#### Scenario: Duplicate, unavailable or overflowing salvage
- **WHEN** a repeated, out-of-reach, damaged-arm, unaffordable or overflowing recovery is requested
- **THEN** it is rejected without partial costs or duplicate output
