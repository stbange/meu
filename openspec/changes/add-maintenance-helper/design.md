# Maintenance design
The deliberately induced grid surge marks a shared distribution-buffer fault. Symptoms precede diagnosis. Isolation is required to queue work; one alloy and two conductor are atomically reserved. Duplicate jobs reject. Cancellation returns reserved stock once. Work advances over 600 active ticks while isolated and powered, spending one kJ per active working second; spent energy is not refunded. Unpowered/unisolated work stays blocked and explicitly described. Completion consumes reserved parts and clears both the underlying fault and overload. Schema-2 fields persist stock, diagnosis, job and progress; pause/load never advances a job. Doors, bridge access and the lift are independent of this bus.
